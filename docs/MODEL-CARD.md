## 1. Model Overview

Smart Voicemail Router (SVR) converts single-speaker Egyptian Arabic telecom customer-care voicemails into structured, explainable callback tickets. The production AI pipeline uses three self-hosted model families running locally in sequential low-memory stages with zero third-party AI API calls:

| Pipeline Stage | Model Artifact | Architecture | Input | Output |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Speech-to-Text (STT) | `openai/whisper-large-v3` (self-hosted via `faster-whisper`) | Encoder-decoder transformer ASR (`beam_size=3`, loudness-normalized) | 16 kHz mono 16-bit PCM WAV (decoded from WAV, MP3, or M4A, up to 60 s) | Raw Arabic/telecom transcript, normalized `text_norm`, and locked 10-column STT v1 metadata |
| Transcript Audit & Repair | `qwen2.5:3b` (local Ollama on `127.0.0.1:11434`) | **LangGraph** `StateGraph` (`AgenticTranscriptGate`) | Raw STT transcript + acoustic/lexical signals | Verified/repaired Arabic transcript, word-level change diff, round trace, or `uncertain_transcript` escalation |
| Complaint Category | `UBC-NLP/MARBERTv2` (single-task checkpoint 1 of 4) | Fine-tuned Arabic BERT encoder + 11-class head | `text_norm` (max 128 tokens) | Predicted label + 11 class probabilities (sum = `1.0 +/- 0.001`) |
| Complaint Urgency | `UBC-NLP/MARBERTv2` (single-task checkpoint 2 of 4) | Fine-tuned Arabic BERT encoder + 3-class head | `text_norm` (max 128 tokens) | Predicted label + 3 class probabilities (sum = `1.0 +/- 0.001`) |
| Customer State | `UBC-NLP/MARBERTv2` (single-task checkpoint 3 of 4) | Fine-tuned Arabic BERT encoder + 4-class head | `text_norm` (max 128 tokens) | Predicted label + 4 class probabilities (sum = `1.0 +/- 0.001`) |
| Churn Risk | `UBC-NLP/MARBERTv2` (single-task checkpoint 4 of 4) | Fine-tuned Arabic BERT encoder + 2-class head | `text_norm` (max 128 tokens) | Predicted label + 2 class probabilities (sum = `1.0 +/- 0.001`) |

Downstream queue priority (`priority_score` 1 to 6, buckets `urgent`, `high`, `normal`, `low`), telecom skill routing (`Network`, `Billing`, `General`), and callback briefs (`summary = transcript[:200]`, category-mapped `customer_request`, and template `suggested_script`) are **100% deterministic rules**, never generated by a language model (PRD Sections 2.2, 8.3, 8.4).

## 2. Intended Use and Out-of-Scope Uses

### 2.1 Intended Use

- **Primary Domain:** Telecom customer-care operations handling single-speaker Egyptian Arabic subscriber voicemails that may include English telecom terms (e.g., SIM, eSIM, 4G, router, bundle, MB, GB).
- **Primary Users:** Telecom customer-care supervisors (queue triage, quality oversight, label correction, human review resolution) and callback agents (prepared callbacks with structured briefs).
- **Operational Mode:** Human-in-the-loop decision support. The models predict four independent fields and confidence scores; fixed rules compute priority and route to eligible agents, while humans execute every customer callback and hold full override and kill-switch control.

### 2.2 Out-of-Scope Uses and Rejected Alternatives

- **Audio Emotion Inference (Closed 2026-11-17):** Direct speech emotion classification was experimentally evaluated and formally closed on `2026-11-17`. Audio embeddings carried no usable emotion signal on this corpus (silhouette score `-0.10`; 4-class audio accuracy approximately `37%`, below the approximately `40%` `calm` majority baseline). Production `customer_state` comes strictly from text (`text_norm`) only (PRD Section 6.5, L4).
- **Multi-Task Kendall Model (Rejected for Production):** A shared-encoder multi-task MARBERTv2 model with Kendall uncertainty weighting achieved a 5-seed mean validation macro-F1 of `0.75536`, underperforming the single-task MARBERTv2 baseline (`0.7708`). Multi-task serving is excluded from MVP production (PRD Section 6.5, AI1, L5).
- **Autonomous Customer Actions:** The system must never dial customers, send SMS/WhatsApp/email replies, issue billing refunds, or mutate telecom subscriber accounts automatically (PRD S1).
- **Non-Target Audio or Dialects:** Multi-speaker live phone calls, clips longer than 60 seconds, non-Egyptian Arabic dialects, or non-telecom domains are out of scope unless validated on a new domain evaluation set (PRD L3, L14).
- **External Cloud AI Processing:** Raw audio, transcripts, and tickets must never be transmitted to third-party AI APIs by default (PRD AI7, Section 10.2).

## 3. Training Data and Frozen Split

The classification models were trained and validated on the **SVR Voicemail Corpus** (`dataset_version: v2`, `split_seed: 42`, stratified `70/20/10` split):

| Corpus Stage | Count | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Raw collected voicemails | 6,522 clips (12.63 hours) | 16 kHz mono WAV, simulated recordings collected with contributor consent (acted emotions, no real customer PII) |
| Whisper `large-v3` transcripts | 6,024 transcripts | Locked 10-column STT v1 format; Latin telecom tokens present in 41 of 6,024 rows |
| Excluded low-quality transcripts | 655 rows | Excluded where Whisper `min_avg_logprob < -0.30` |
| **Frozen Clean Dataset (`v2`)** | **5,096 rows** | **Train: 3,575 / Validation: 1,003 / Test: 518** |

### 3.1 Label Taxonomy (PRD Appendix B)

1. **`complaint_category` (11 classes):** `network_and_connectivity`, `billing_and_charges`, `balance_and_data`, `app_and_account`, `branch_and_staff`, `no_reply_or_long_wait`, `cancellation_request`, `thanks_or_praise`, `other`, `offers_and_packages`, `sim_services` (`greeting_only` excluded with 0 rows).
2. **`complaint_urgency` (3 classes):** `high`, `medium`, `low` (assessed from the complaint severity and time sensitivity only, never from vocal tone).
3. **`customer_state` (4 trained classes):** `calm`, `frustrated`, `mad`, `satisfied`. The fifth label `unclear` has 0 rows in train, 1 row in validation, and 0 rows in test; it is excluded from the `customer_state` macro-F1 denominator (`n_scored = 1,002`).
4. **`churn_risk` (2 classes):** `at_risk`, `not_at_risk` (explicit departure language only; `at_risk` represents `0.56%` of train with 20 train rows and 7 validation rows).

### 3.2 Label Provenance Notice (L2, S4)

Initial dataset labels were generated by an automated ML annotation pipeline and remain subject to ongoing human audit. Accordingly, every automated prediction in production is labeled as `model` provenance and stored separately from `human` supervisor corrections (`Ticket.label_sources`), which feed future retraining datasets once audited.

## 4. Training Protocol and Hyperparameters

All four single-task classifiers follow the locked SVR training protocol (`AI1`, `runs/fair-comparison-2026-11-19/train_fair.py`, `notebooks/training/shared_config.json`):

- **Base Backbone:** `UBC-NLP/MARBERTv2` (one independent sequence classification model per field)
- **Input Representation:** Normalized transcript `text_norm`, truncated to `max_seq_len = 128`
- **Optimizer and Schedule:** `AdamW`, learning rate `2e-5`, warmup ratio `0.1`, weight decay `0.01`, dropout `0.1`
- **Batch Size and Epochs:** Effective batch size `16`, `3` epochs (max `5` with early stopping patience `2`), selecting the highest validation macro-F1 checkpoint
- **Multi-Seed Verification:** 5 fixed seeds (`42, 1337, 2023, 7, 99`), reporting mean and sample standard deviation (`ddof=1`)
- **Learning Rate Selection:** Seed 42 sweep yielded `1e-5: 0.6760`, `2e-5: 0.7661`, `3e-5: 0.7778`. Learning rate `2e-5` was selected for production because the `3e-5` delta rested on a single churn row while `2e-5` outperformed on the other three fields (PRD Appendix A).
- **Imbalance Handling (Train-Only Class Weights):** Preprocessing does not oversample or undersample the dataset. Balanced class weights are computed strictly from `final/clean_training_train.csv` (3,575 rows) via `sklearn.utils.class_weight.compute_class_weight("balanced")` and stored in `final/class_weights.json`:
  - `churn_risk`: `at_risk = 89.375`, `not_at_risk = 0.503`
  - `customer_state`: `calm = 0.617`, `frustrated = 0.567`, `mad = 8.677`, `satisfied = 1.999`
  - `complaint_urgency`: `high = 3.484`, `medium = 0.556`, `low = 1.094`
  - `complaint_category`: `network_and_connectivity = 0.544`, `billing_and_charges = 0.673`, `balance_and_data = 0.678`, `app_and_account = 0.711`, `thanks_or_praise = 0.734`, `no_reply_or_long_wait = 1.094`, `branch_and_staff = 1.102`, `other = 1.451`, `cancellation_request = 2.444`, `offers_and_packages = 2.642`, `sim_services = 7.386`

### 5.3 Test-Once Evaluation Discipline (AI9, L7)

The frozen test split (`final/clean_training_test.csv`, 518 rows) is never used for training, hyperparameter tuning, or threshold calibration. Production evaluation enforces a SHA-256 once-guard (`backend/svr_platform/eval/once_guard.py`) that records an immutable claim in `runs/test_evaluations.json` before opening test labels and refuses repeat evaluations for the same `(model_version, dataset_version)` tuple.

### 5.4 Speech-to-Text (`openai/whisper-large-v3`) & VoiceGate Audit (`qwen2.5:3b`)

- Self-hosted via `faster-whisper` (`language="ar"`, `beam_size=3`, `vad_filter=True`), decoding uploads into 16 kHz mono 16-bit PCM WAV with safe RMS/peak loudness normalization (`normalize_wav_16k_loudness()`) for quiet microphone recordings.
- Records all 10 locked STT v1 fields (`file`, `model`, `text`, `text_norm`, `min_avg_logprob`, `max_no_speech_prob`, `needs_review`, `review_reasons`, `dup_text_size`, `error`).
- **VoiceGate Orchestrator (`AgenticTranscriptGate`)** runs a LangGraph `StateGraph` (`pre_repair -> audit -> confidence_check -> finalize`) using local Ollama `qwen2.5:3b` (loopback-only, or internal Docker `ollama` service when `SVR_ALLOW_DOCKER_OLLAMA=1`) to remove Whisper stutter duplicates, repair Egyptian Arabic telecom ASR errors, and block hallucinations (CJK leakage, digit invention, Latin injection, word-order drift). A clean-transcript fast-path (`AGENTIC_GATE_FAST_PATH_ENABLED=true`) bypasses LLM rounds when `min_avg_logprob >= -0.35` and no suspect tokens or pre-repairs are detected.
- **CPU Acceleration (`SVR_MARBERT_BACKEND=auto`):** `ops/export_marbert_onnx.py --seed 99` exports the 4 MARBERTv2 heads to ONNX INT8 (`model_int8.onnx`, 377.7 MB each), achieving **2.18x CPU speedup** (`p50=105.3 ms`, `p95=170.8 ms` vs PyTorch `229.8 ms` / `374.1 ms` warm) while passing all 4 AI9 validation gates on `1,003` rows (`category=0.7847`, `urgency=0.7055`, `state=0.7487`, `churn=0.9162`, `combined=0.7888`). `Whisper large-v3` (`int8`, `beam_size=3`) remains the default STT model (`26.49 s` mean CPU latency on 5 validation WAVs; `Systran/faster-whisper-large-v3-turbo` returned 401 Repository Not Found).
- Flags low-confidence transcripts (`min_avg_logprob < -0.50`), severe acoustic degradation (`min_avg_logprob < -0.75`), high silence (`max_no_speech_prob > 0.80`), empty transcripts, or single-token transcripts for Human Review (`uncertain_transcript`).
- Retries up to 3 times with backoff; on failure, degrades safely to the Human Review Queue (`processing_error`) without losing the voicemail (NFR14).

## 6. Known Limitations (PRD Section 13, L1 to L8)

| ID | Limitation | Operational Impact | Mitigation in Production |
|---|---|---|---|
| **L1** | Probabilistic outputs can vary across random seeds and misclassify edge cases | Occasional incorrect category, urgency, state, or churn label | Ticket confidence `min(p_i)` gated against threshold (`0.5`), Human Review Queue (AI3), 5% weekly supervisor sampling (AI10) |
| **L2** | Training labels originate from an ML pipeline and are undergoing ongoing human audit | Model may reproduce systematic label errors until audited retraining | Model outputs explicitly tagged as `model` vs `human` (S4); supervisor corrections feed audited retraining set |
| **L3** | Validated exclusively on simulated single-speaker Egyptian Arabic telecom voicemails | Accuracy may drop on other Arabic dialects, multi-speaker recordings, or noisy real telephony lines | Unsupported or low-confidence inputs route to Human Review; domain validation required before expanding dialects/channels |
| **L4** | Audio emotion inference is not used (closed 2026-11-17; silhouette `-0.10`, 4-class ~37% vs ~40% calm baseline) | Vocal tone, shouting, or sarcasm absent from text is not captured | Customer state relies on validated text cues; agents still have authenticated audio playback on every ticket |
| **L5** | Multi-task Kendall model underperforms single-task (`0.75536` vs `0.7708`) | Production serves 4 separate MARBERTv2 checkpoints, increasing memory footprint | Single-task checkpoints served in parallel worker replicas (NFR9); multi-task reconsidered only if it beats single-task on validation |
| **L6** | Churn validation rests on only 7 `at_risk` rows (high variance across seeds) | Single-row flips swing churn macro-F1 noticeably | Evaluation reports always display per-seed churn F1 and `at_risk` count; supervisors monitor cancellation and churn tickets closely |
| **L7** | Frozen test split (518 rows) is evaluated once under a SHA-256 guard | Single-shot test estimate cannot be repeatedly resampled | 5-seed validation mean and std are always reported alongside the guarded test score |
| **L8** | Latency target (`p95 <= 10 s` for 60 s audio) requires GPU acceleration | CPU-only hosts exceed the 10 s p95 latency budget | Production deployment targets GPU inference servers (A2, NFR22); CPU mode is documented with relaxed latency and monitored via Prometheus/Grafana |

## 7. Human-in-the-Loop Oversight and Safety Guardrails

1. **Confidence Gating (AI3):** Ticket confidence equals the minimum predicted-class probability across the four independent fields (`confidence = min(p_category, p_urgency, p_state, p_churn)`). Any ticket below the active threshold (default `0.5`, snapshotted at creation time per FR21) enters the Human Review Queue.
2. **Taxonomy and Probability Validation (FR6, FR8, S3):** Every field probability vector must cover the exact trained classes and sum to `1.0 +/- 0.001`. Any missing or out-of-taxonomy label is rejected and forces Human Review.
3. **Null-Input Priority Guard (FR9):** If any priority input (`complaint_urgency`, `churn_risk`, `customer_state`) is null, `priority_score` and `queue_priority` remain null and the ticket routes to Human Review.
4. **Supervisor Correction Preview and Provenance (WF2, FR13, S4):** Supervisors see recomputed priority and agent routing before confirming any label edit. Confirmed edits flip `label_sources[field]` from `"model"` to `"human"` and log immutable `old_value` and `new_value` audit diffs.
5. **Immediate Kill Switch (Section 10.5):** Admins or supervisors can toggle automatic routing off immediately; while disabled, every new ticket enters the Human Review Queue (`kill_switch_active`).
6. **Continuous Monitoring and Alerts (AI10, NFR20):** Prometheus and Grafana track per-stage latency (`transcribe`, `classify`, `priority`, `route`, `brief`), classification distributions, confidence distributions, and fire alerts if human review rate exceeds `20%` or p95 pipeline latency exceeds `10 s` over 24 hours. At least `5%` of ready tickets are deterministically sampled for weekly supervisor quality review.
